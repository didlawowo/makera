Makera Z1 Low-Profile Vise Quick Start

¶ Makera Z1 Low-Profile Vise Quick Start Guide

https://youtu.be/Iqy27HT48Kw

1. Installation: 

1.1 Home the machine in the control software, then power it off (spindle at the far right, table at the front) 

1.2 Remove any tool from the spindle 

1.3 Remove the MDF wasteboard 

1.4 Insert two 4×11 mm locating pins 

1.5 Position the low-profile vise on the locating pins and remove the left fixed jaw using the included hex key 

1.6 Secure the vise with six M5×20 screws, then reinstall the fixed jaw (ensure the locating key is properly seated)

2. Workpiece Clamping:  

2.1 Loosen the screw at position 3 on the movable jaw 

2.2 Adjust the screw at position 2 to bring the movable jaw closer to the jaw carrier 

2.3 Loosen the screw at position 1 on the jaw carrier 

2.4 Open the jaws and insert the workpiece 

2.5 Tighten the screw at position 1 

2.6 Adjust the screw at position 2 to clamp the workpiece 

2.7 Tighten the screw at position 3

Note: 

The vise is equipped with slotted soft jaws by default, suitable for thin workpieces. For taller parts, clamp directly against the bottom of the jaws for better stability.
