Replacing the Top Polycarbonate Cover of the Carvera Air

¶ Replacing the Top Poly-carbonate Cover of the Carvera Air

¶ Tools Required

  • 2mm Allen key
  • Sticky tape

¶ Preparation

Secure the Top Button – Before starting, place a piece of sticky tape over the top button to prevent it from falling out during the process (Figure 1).

¶ Disassembly Process

¶ Step 1: Remove the Screws

  1. Using the 2mm Allen key, remove the screws securing the top poly-carbonate cover (Figure 2).
  1. Manually move the spindle to gain access to all screws. For safety purposes, ensure that the machine is powered off before attempting this step  (Figure 3).

¶ Step 2: Remove the Side Screws

  1. Remove the screws on the left side of the cover (Figure 3).
  1. The screws on the right side are identical to those on the left—remove them as well (Figure 4).

¶ Installation Process

¶ Step 3: Replace the Poly-carbonate Cover

  1. Carefully remove the old cover by slightly flexing the poly-carbonate sheet to unhook it from its seated position.
  2. Position the new poly-carbonate cover and gently flex it as needed to align it with the mounting points.
  3. Secure the cover by reinstalling and tightening all screws.
  4. Remove the sticky tape from the top button and ensure it remains correctly positioned.

¶ Final Check

  • Ensure the new cover is firmly secured and properly seated.
  • Verify that the top button remains functional and correctly positioned.

The replacement process is now complete, and the Carvera Air is ready for use.
