Carvera Air Troubleshooting

¶ Carvera Air general preliminary troubleshooting

Please note that this page is a WOP and Makera reserves the right to modify and update content accordingly.

 

When an error occurs on the machine it can be mostly solved by following the steps below:

1. Have you tried it a second time?

2. Is your firmware and the controller software updated?

3. Have you unplugged all cables and reset the machine?

4. Are you using the right post processor?

 

If the above does not solve the problem, further investigation is needed. Below are a few general troubleshooting topics.
