¶ Introduction

Practice is the most direct and effective learning method. We provide a series of machining samples, materials and tools to make you familiar with the functions and procedures of using Carvera Air to do delicate works.

With streamlined capabilities, Carvera Air greatly simplifies the machining process. However, we still recommend reading the Carvera Air instruction manual carefully before start using it. This will ensure that you get a quick knowledge of Carvera Air and avoid basic misoperations.
