Makera Z1 Maintenance

  • The Makera Z1 CNC machine is a complex electromechanical device full of sensors, electronics, and mechanical parts.
  • Parts do wear and faults are unavoidable during its long service.
  • Regular maintenance will reduce the wear and increase the life of the CNC machine.
  • Before starting any maintenance procedures ensure the machine is turned off and unplugged.
  • Vacuum or compressed air can be used for cleaning.
