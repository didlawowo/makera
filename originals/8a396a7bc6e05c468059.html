Usage of External Control

¶ Usage of External Control interfaces:

1. The External Control pin is located on the back panel of the machine and connected through an aviation plug (GX12-4).

 

2. Instructions for aviation plug pins

3. Drive the opening and closing of high-power loads with external relays.

        we can connect the ‘external control output’ to the coil pins of a 24V relay, and then connect the high-power load to the output pins of the relay. When entering the "M851 S100" command in the MDI window of the controller, the relay output node will be closed. After entering the "M852" command, the relay output node will be opened.
