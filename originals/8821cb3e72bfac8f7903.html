¶ Email Verification

The first time you use the software, you will need to verify your email. We have enrolled all customer emails in the system.

For Customers

  • If you've purchased Carvera or participated in the Carvera Air crowdfunding, verify and log in using your purchase email.
  • If you encounter issues with email authentication, please contact us at info@makera.com.

For a FREE trial:

If you're not a current customer but would like to try Makera CAM, we offer a 15-day trial period for users to explore the software’s features before committing to a purchase. After downloading Makera CAM, enter your email to begin the 15-day trial period, and contact us at info@makera.com for questions and support.
