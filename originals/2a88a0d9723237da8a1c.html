Makera 3D Probe

¶ Makera 3D Probe Quick Start Guide

https://youtu.be/_fKaCgN8EAE

The 3D probe enables automatic X / Y / Z probing on both conductive and non-conductive materials. It is ideal for edge finding and center finding on regular shapes (such as square or round workpieces), especially when using custom fixtures like vises.

Operation: 

1. Follow the on-screen instructions to switch to the 3D probe 

2. Remove the current tool and install the 3D probe 

3. Unplug the wired probe connector and connect the 3D probe cable 

4. Tap ‘Continue’ or press the main button to start automatic calibration 

5. Follow the on-screen guidance to perform probing process

Note: 

1. After changing the spindle collet, use the appropriate adapter sleeve 

2. The probe is factory-calibrated. Recalibration is required after collisions or when changing the probe holder (see wiki.makera.com)
