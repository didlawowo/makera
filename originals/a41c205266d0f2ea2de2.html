Installation： 

1. Install the engraving module into the spindle collet as you would a standard tool. Ensure the orientation matches the illustration (label facing outward). 

2. Connect the laser module cable to the 3-pin port on the right side of the spindle housing. 

3. Use the included silicone tube to connect the air inlet of the engraving module to the machine’s air outlet to enable air assist (Optional, Carvera Air only).

 

Note: 

1. After installation, ensure the engraving module is securely tightened and cannot rotate 

2. Make sure the workpiece surface is flat and free of interference 

3. Always wear laser safety goggles during operation
