Cyclone Dust Collector Maintenance

  • The Makera Cyclone Dust Collector is a complex electromechanical device full of sensors, electronics, and mechanical parts.
  • Parts do wear and faults are unavoidable during its long service.
  • Regular maintenance will reduce the wear and increase the life of the Dust Collector.
  • Before starting any maintenance procedures ensure the Dust Collector is turned off and unplugged.
  • Vacuum or compressed air can be used for cleaning, do not wash filters or parts.
