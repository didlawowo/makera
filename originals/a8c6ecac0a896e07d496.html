Makera Z1 Vacuum Bed Quick Start

¶ Makera Z1 Vacuum Bed Quick Start Guide

https://youtu.be/TU1L6R7Vlto

1. Preparation: Connect the two suction tubes to the air inlets on both sides of the vacuum table as shown.

2. Installation: 

2.1 Home the machine in the control software, then power it off (spindle at the far right, machine bed at the front) 

2.2 Remove any tool from the spindle to avoid injury 

2.3 Remove the MDF wasteboard 

2.4 Insert two 4×11 mm locating pins as shown 

2.5 Position the vacuum table using the locating pins, secure it with four M5×20 screws, and install the dedicated MDF board 

2.6 Route the suction tubes from both sides through the openings under the machine, and connect them to the rear air ports 

2.7 Remove the air tubes from the internal rear air inlets and replace them with the suction tubes (fix removed tubes with the provided plugs) 

2.8 Insert the suction hose of the Makera dust collector into the upper rear air port

Note: 

1. The vacuum table is suitable for thin, flat materials such as paper, bicolor sheet, and PCBs. Do not use it for thick or metal workpieces。

2. Due to the permeability of the MDF board, suction may be insufficient for small workpieces. Cover unused areas with paper or plastic film to improve suction.
