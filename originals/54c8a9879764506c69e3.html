Collet Lever

¶ Fixing a Loose Collet Lever

¶ Tools Required

  • 1.5mm Allen key
  • 2.5mm Allen key

¶ Procedure

¶ Step 1: Remove the Grub Screws

  1. Locate the grub screws securing the collet lever shaft.
  2. Using a 1.5mm Allen key, remove the front grub screw as shown in Figure 1.
  3. Similarly, remove the rear grub screw as indicated in Figure 2.

¶ Step 2: Remove the Collet Release Lever

  1. With the grub screws removed, lift the collet release lever upward and out of the collet lever shaft.
  2. Ensure that no debris or obstructions are present before proceeding to the next step.

¶ Step 3: Tighten the Shaft Securing Screw

  1. Looking from the left side, locate the central hole in the collet lever shaft.
  2. Inside this hole, identify the 2.5mm Allen screw that secures the shaft in place (Figure 3).
  1. Use a 2.5mm Allen key to tighten this screw, ensuring the shaft is firmly secured.

¶ Step 4: Reinstall the Collet Release Lever

  1. Position the collet release lever back onto the shaft.
  2. Insert and tighten the grub screws, ensuring they are properly seated in the small locating hole near the end of the shaft (Figure 4).

¶ Final Check

  • Confirm that the collet lever moves freely without excessive play.
  • Ensure all screws are securely fastened to prevent future loosening.
  • Test the functionality before returning the machine to operation.
