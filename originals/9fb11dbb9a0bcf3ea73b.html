¶ Milling Spindle - 3.175mm (1/8") Shank Diameter Bits

The following recommended parameters are based on current tests. The machining speed with small diameter tools/hard materials should be slow and fast in opposite. We will conduct more tests and provide more detailed parameter recommendations in the future on our website.

Please start the test from the lower limit of the parameter, and adjust them based on the test results. For additional information and support, check out our Getting Started with CNC Machines tutorials and guides.

¶ Laser Module - Carvera (2.5w) and Carvera Air (5w)

Note that these settings are estimates as specific material properties can vary based on material color and manufacturer. Always take extreme care when testing laser settings, perform test cuts in a well ventilated space, always use protective eyewear, and never leave the laser unattended. Also ensure that the materials being cut and engraved are compatible with laser processing, and if not, utilize the milling spindle instead. 

For additional information and support, check out our Carvera How To's and Carvera Air How To's for tutorials and guides.
