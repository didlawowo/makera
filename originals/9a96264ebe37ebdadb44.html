¶ Cyclone Dust Collector general preliminary troubleshooting

Please note that this page is a WOP and Makera reserves the right to modify and update content accordingly.

 

When an error occurs with the unit it can be mostly solved by following the steps below:

1. Have you tried it a second time?

2. Are all ports and cables connected properly?

3. Have you unplugged all cables and reset the unit?

4. Have you checked for clogging?
