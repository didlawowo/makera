Cleaning the Carvera

A clean machine is a happy machine.

  • Use a vacuum or compressed air to clean the machine's surfaces to remove dust, debris, and any accumulated material.
  • Move the X and Y-axis to clean areas under the bed and carriage.
  • Make sure you use a clean cloth when wiping the acrylic cover, Small particles could cause scratches.
  • Pay special attention to the ATC area, any debris might interfere with the ATC process.
  • Clean under the machine, some debris does make its way to the bottom of the machine.
  • Empty the dust bin regularly, and vacuum or use compressed air to clean and unblock the filter mesh.
