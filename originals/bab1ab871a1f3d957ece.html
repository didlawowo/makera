About Us

¶ About Makera

Makera is a tech company that focuses on desktop digital fabrication products. Starting with the Carvera, a one-of-a-kind desktop CNC machine that breaks the barriers between the digital and physical worlds, we are bringing desktop manufacturing to a whole new level. 

¶ Who We Are

We are an experienced team of makers, adept in electronics, machinery, and software.

Our journey began in 2019, with an ambitious goal to create the Carvera — the ultimate CNC machine for makers. Designed to fit right on your desk, empowers you to swiftly transform your ideas into tangible realities.

In 2021, we initiated our first Kickstarter campaign, which was met with overwhelming success. We raised over $1.7 million from more than 500 backers, making the Carvera the top-ranked CNC product on Kickstarter. We extend our profound gratitude to everyone who supported us in this journey.

In early 2023, we successfully delivered machines to all our Kickstarter backers and subsequently launched our website to cater to new orders. With thousands of Carvera machines sold, we are proud to have satisfied customers globally. Our users love the Carvera and we're confident you will too.
